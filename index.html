About HHI Examinations Portal

The HHI examinations portal is a website that helps guardians to easily allocate their child's school report exams online. The development of this site was for the purpose of reducing school report inaccessability. Now the school reports can be accessed easily on your phone and at the comfort of home or any where.

How To Use

1. You can Click "See results" on the welcome section and you can be redirected to a website where you can choose the class of your child or the wanted student
2. You can click the menu and select "results" you shall also be redirected to the same website.

3. Once in that sit you can click the desired class of the student you wish to access the report for.

4. Once you are in the class of the student : a. you can click the search bar and write his or her name and you can see his or her details b. or you can scroll manually and find the results
